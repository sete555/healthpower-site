1. The short version

  • Your records are stored on your iPhone.
  • There are no accounts.
  • The App does not send your records to the provider (the provider runs no server for the App).
  • There is no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking. The App contains no SDKs for these.
  • Your records are not sold or given to anyone.
  • For Apple Health, the App handles only the items you allow. Every item is optional and can be stopped later.
  • iCloud sync between your devices is not in this version (v1.0). If it is added later, it will use only your own iCloud and will not include information that came from Apple Health (see Section 6).
  • If you use the Apple Watch app, information moves only between your iPhone and your Apple Watch (an OS mechanism), never through a provider server (see Section 3).

3. Transmission outside your device

  • The App has no feature that sends your records to servers on the internet, and contains no networking SDKs.
  • Communication with Apple Health happens inside your iPhone.
  • Notifications (end of rest timer, supplement reminders) are produced only on your iPhone.
  • Apple Watch app: when you start a workout on the iPhone, the paired Apple Watch app measures a strength-training workout and saves heart rate and energy to Apple Health. Live heart rate is passed to the iPhone only between the iPhone and the Watch (an OS mechanism), never through a provider server. The Watch app runs only during a workout. The iPhone app works without a Watch.
  • Live Activity (Lock Screen / Dynamic Island): during a workout it shows records you entered (exercise, set progress, elapsed time, rest remaining). It runs only on your iPhone, with no push service or server, and can be turned off in iOS Settings.
  • Background audio: only when you turn on "Play with the screen off" in Settings (off by default), the App plays silent audio during a rest interval to keep the timer running and a short beep when it ends. It does not record sound or collect audio data.
  • In the following cases, another app or service opens only when you tap. After that, that service's own privacy policy applies, and the provider cannot control it.
  • Tapping "Search tutorials on YouTube" on an exercise screen (the search words are passed to YouTube in your browser)
  • Tapping a link you pasted in an exercise note
  • When you export, the file is handed to the destination you choose (Files, AirDrop, another app, etc.). See Section 7.

4. The "import an AI app's answer" feature

  • The App has no AI inside. The App sends nothing to any AI.
  • You can show a meal photo to an AI app yourself and paste the answer (text) into HealthPower. Only then does the App check the text, show it to you, and save it only when you tap "Import". The same applies when text is passed via Shortcuts or Siri (a confirmation screen always appears before saving).
  • The App does not handle photos.
  • What you send to an AI app is your decision. AI apps and their providers handle information under their own terms and privacy policies, which the provider of this App cannot control. Check those terms before you give health-related information to an AI app.
  • AI answers are rough estimates and can be wrong. Imported meals are marked "estimate" and can be deleted later.

5. Third parties, advertising, analytics

  • Your information does not reach the provider, so it cannot be shared with or sold to third parties.
  • The App shows no ads. It does not analyze usage or track your behavior.
  • Note: through Apple mechanisms (crash reports, TestFlight feedback, etc.), the provider may receive diagnostic information via Apple only if you choose to share it. That is governed by Apple's privacy policy.
  • Purchase is a one-time App Store purchase. Apple handles payment information; it does not reach the provider.

  • Not synced: information read from Apple Health (weight, body fat, sleep, steps, active energy, etc.) and daily targets calculated from it, and the "Today's HP" scores, are not placed in iCloud (also not in v1.1); each device reads Health again. 7. Storage, export, restore, deletion

  • Where: on your iPhone, in the App's own storage area.
  • Protection: covered by iOS's standard data protection (tied to your passcode). The App does not apply its own encryption.
  • In-app automatic backup: when you open the App, it saves a copy of the last 7 days on your iPhone once a day, so you do not lose data when the App is updated. Records imported from Apple Health (weight, sleep, etc.) are not included (they are re-imported from Health after a restore). The file protection level lets it be read after the device is first unlocked. "Delete all data" also removes this copy. It is not sent anywhere.
  • Device backups: depending on your iPhone settings, iOS may include the App's data in backups (iCloud Backup, computer backup). [CONFIRM the scope; OPEN_QUESTIONS.md item 6]
  • Export: in Settings > Data & Privacy, you can take out all data as JSON or CSV. The format, fields and units are visible on the "Data fields and units" screen. You can choose whether to include records imported from Apple Health (default: included). Exported files are not encrypted. You choose the destination and are responsible for how the file is handled afterwards.
  • Restore: you can restore from a JSON file exported by the App. It does not delete current data, and importing the same file twice does not create duplicates. It can be used when you change phones.
  • Deletion: you can delete individual records at any time. "Delete all data" in Data & Privacy removes everything (this cannot be undone), with a choice of whether to also remove what the App wrote to Health. Deleting the App removes the data on the iPhone.
  • Please note: because the provider does not hold your data, it cannot restore it if your device is lost or broken. Please save exports regularly.

8. Medical notice

  • Goals and numbers in the App are estimates based on what you enter and general formulas. They are not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.
  • "Today's HP" is a game score calculated on your device from sleep, activity, workouts, food and water. It is not a diagnosis or assessment of your health, nor medical advice.
  • Maximum heart rate on heart-rate screens is an estimated guide. Heart-rate intensity (time at high heart rate, peak) is a reference figure calculated from the heart rate read, not an evaluation or diagnosis of exercise strength or your condition, nor medical advice.
  • Nutrient reference values are general values for healthy adults, not your personal requirement or medical advice.
  • If you have health concerns or a medical condition, are pregnant or nursing, or struggle with eating, consult a doctor or registered dietitian.
  • Automatic food-target calculation is not provided for users under 18 (you can still set targets by hand).
  • Safety rules (such as the lower limit on calorie targets) are shown in the App. The full wording is in DISCLAIMER.md.
